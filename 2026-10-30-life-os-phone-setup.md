# Put Life OS on your phone

Do these steps one time.

## 1. Publish the app
1. On GitHub, open the `crowded-mummy` repo.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Pages**.
3. Under **Source**, pick **GitHub Actions**.
   - If you do not see this, your plan may not allow Pages on a private repo. Tell Claude. There is a free fallback.
4. Merge the branch `claude/life-os-implementation-h7oaa9` into `main`.
5. Open the **Actions** tab. Wait for "Deploy app" to turn green.
6. Open **Settings**, then **Pages**. Your web address is shown there.
   It looks like `https://vindurrin.github.io/crowded-mummy/`.

Only the app code is published. Your seed file is not.

## 2. Add the app to your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the web address. Tap Share. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the web address. Tap the menu. Tap **Install app**.

## 3. Load your plan (one time)
1. Get the seed file on your phone (for example in iCloud Drive or Files).
   The file is `2026-10-30-life-os-seed-data.json`.
2. Open Life OS. Tap **Load seed file**. Pick the file.
3. Answer the questions on the Today screen. You can skip any of them.

## Keep your data safe
- Your data lives only on this phone.
- Open **Admin**. Tap **Export backup** each week. Keep the file in iCloud Drive.
- If you use Safari, add the app to the Home Screen. Safari can delete data of sites you do not use for a while. A Home Screen app is safer.
